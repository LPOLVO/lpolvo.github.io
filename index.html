Frequently Asked Questions
Are the scripts safe to use?▼

All scripts are manually reviewed by our security team. However, we always recommend using scripts on alternate accounts to keep your main account safe. We are not responsible for any bans.

How do I get a premium key?▼

Click the "Buy Key" button in the top banner or visit our Discord server. Keys are available starting at $4.99 for 30 days of access to all premium scripts.

Can I upload my own scripts?▼

Yes! Any registered user can upload scripts. They go through a review process and are typically approved within 24 hours. Top contributors get featured on the homepage.

How do I change my profile avatar?▼

Sign in and click your name in the top right. Select "Edit Profile" and click the Upload Photo button. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF (max 2MB).

Why can I only change my name once per week?▼

The weekly name change limit prevents abuse and helps the community recognize established members. This limit may change for VIP and Admin accounts.

Is LPOLVO Scripts Hub affiliated with Roblox Corporation?▼

No. LPOLVO Scripts Hub is an independent community platform. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Roblox Corporation in any way.

What executor should I use?▼

We recommend Delta, Arceus X, or Fluxus for mobile. For PC, try Synapse Z, Script-Ware, or Wave. Always download executors from their official websites only.
