FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LPOLVO HUB scripts.

Are these scripts safe to use? +
All scripts on LPOLVO HUB are personally reviewed and tested before publishing. However, using any third-party scripts in Roblox always carries some risk of account action. Use with a secondary account when trying new scripts.
Do I need a key for every script? +
Some scripts are completely free and require no key. Premium scripts require a key that you can get from our Discord server. Keys are valid for 24 hours and can be regenerated for free.
Which executor should I use? +
We recommend Synapse X for the best compatibility. KRNL and Delta are free alternatives that work with most of our scripts. Always download executors from their official websites only.
How often are scripts updated? +
Scripts are updated regularly whenever Roblox pushes major game patches. We usually have updates live within 24 hours. Follow our Discord for instant update notifications.
Can I submit my own scripts? +
Yes! Use the Upload page to submit your scripts for review. Approved scripts will be featured on the Community Scripts page. All submissions are reviewed for safety before publishing.
Is LPOLVO HUB free to use? +
Yes, LPOLVO HUB is completely free. We offer a selection of premium scripts that require a free key from Discord. There are no paid subscriptions or hidden fees.
